How moderation works

Posts go live immediately — there is no pre-publication review and nothing waits here to be published. When a video is reported for breaking the rules (music, women, haram content), it is restricted at once: it leaves the feed and lands in the Restricted tab. Check it there, then Delete it or Restore it. Every restricted video is 1 strike; at 3 strikes the account is suspended automatically. The Moderation Queue below only holds older videos that were pending before this change. You can still Approve, Reject (with a reason the creator will see), or mark Needs review on those.

Restricted — reported videos

A video lands here the moment someone reports it (it leaves the feed at once). Check it, then Delete it or Restore it. Delete removes the video and tells the creator why, with the strikes they have left. Restore puts it back live and clears that strike. Every restricted video is 1 strike; at 3 strikes the account is suspended automatically (restore it from the Users tab if that was a mistake). A direct Delete from anywhere else carries no strike — restrict the video first when it should count as one.

Add a sound

Only sound effects, naats, taqreers and other Islamic audio. No music, no instruments, no songs. Add only audio you own or have rights to use. Sounds are also added by users in the app and go live at once — there is no approval step. Original sounds from videos appear in the list too. When a sound is reported for breaking the rules, remove it here.
