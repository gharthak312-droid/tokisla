How moderation works

Posts go live immediately — there is no pre-publication review and nothing waits here to be published. When a video is reported for breaking the rules (music, women, haram content), act on it in the Reports tab: remove it and apply a strike. The Moderation Queue below only holds older videos that were pending before this change. You can still Approve, Reject (with a reason the creator will see), or mark Needs review on those.

Add a sound

Only sound effects, naats, taqreers and other Islamic audio. No music, no instruments, no songs. Add only audio you own or have rights to use. Sounds are also added by users in the app and go live at once — there is no approval step. Original sounds from videos appear in the list too. When a sound is reported for breaking the rules, remove it here.
